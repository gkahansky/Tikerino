Principles

What "clean, young, fun — not heavy" means in practice.

1
One idea per screenIf a phone screen needs a scrollbar, it becomes two screens.
2
The chart is the heroFull-bleed charts, generous whitespace, zero competing chrome.
3
Thumb-firstTap targets ≥48px, actions in the thumb zone, max 3 text styles per screen.
4
Celebrate progressSpring reveals, a haptic tick on correct, confetti reserved for exam passes.
5
Friendly voiceShort sentences. No jargon before it has been taught.

The game loop

One loop = 2–4 minutes.

  • 1
    LearnOne small principle on a single card — ≤60 words + one annotated chart.
  • 2
    Guided exampleThe principle applied to one real historical chart, step by step.
  • 3
    Practice5–10 real charts, cut before the outcome. Mark the pattern or pick an action.
  • 4
    Instant feedbackWhat you saw, what you missed, then the chart animates forward.
  • 5
    ProgressXP, streaks, crowns. Tough exercises return via spaced review.
  • 6
    Market examNo hints, unseen symbols and dates. Pass it to level up.

Name proposals

Indicative web/app-store/DNS checks only — a formal trademark screen is still required before any lock.

CandleCamp low–amber risk

Candlestick + camp: a friendly place you go to train. No app-store or trading-education collision found; panel's preference. Weakest instant "investing" signal.

TickerTrek low–amber risk

A journey through the market. Only non-commercial student projects collide. Strong quest energy.

Chartling high collision

A little creature learning charts. The panel flags ChartLingo.ai — a near-identical chart-learning product — as too close.

Logo directions

Three directions, each paired with its name. Editable SVGs exist behind these previews.

A — The Learner. Friendliest and most mascot-extensible (reactions, outfits, exam judge). The panel's web designer leans here.
B — The Campfire Wick. The candle's wick becomes a campfire flame over crossed logs. Cleverest pun, strongest as a small app icon. The Critic panel's pick.
C — The Trek. A dotted trail climbing like a price line to a summit flag. Most energetic; reads instantly as a journey.
