**Ask (Guy, 19/9):** build a spatial map/path; suggest 2 concepts with mockups to decide. Mockups will be produced in Stitch and attached when access completes. These are the two candidates, written to be judged side by side.

## **Concept A \- The Trail (proven pattern, tuned to Tikerino)**

Shape: one winding vertical trail per module, scrolling bottom-to-top. Lesson nodes sit on the trail as round stepping stones; the next lesson pulses gently. The bull stands at your current stone and walks ahead to the next when you finish a lesson. Milestones (mixed review, market exam) are landmarks on the trail \- a gate for the review, a summit flag for the exam. Between modules the trail crosses a small bridge with the module's name.  
Why it fits: this is the Duolingo pattern that out-tested every alternative; zero learning curve; the winding shape creates natural "just one more" pull; nodes map 1:1 to our content model (lessons, reviews, exams) so Engineering effort is small.  
Bull's role: journey companion on the trail; walks ahead on completion (short 600ms trot), celebrates at the exam flag, waits at your stone otherwise. Never in lessons.  
Vibrant vs professional: colour lives in the trail environment (sky tint shifts per module); nodes and cards stay on the locked palette.  
Risk: it reads as "Duolingo clone" if the art is generic; the bull and the chart-stone styling must carry the brand.

## **Concept B \- The Chart Climb (brand-distinctive)**

Shape: the journey is itself a rising chart. Each module is one upward leg of a long candlestick chart drawn as a mountain ridge; lessons are the candles you climb, left to right, module after module. Your progress literally draws the chart higher \- "your knowledge compounds" made visual. Milestones are marked candles (tall wick for the exam); completed modules form a visible uptrend behind you; a dashed future line shows the path ahead fading out.  
Why it fits: nobody else has it; it is unmistakably Tikerino (the path teaches the product's core metaphor); gives the "destination" framing that named journeys need (the summit \= the capstone); screenshot-worthy, which helps word of mouth.  
Bull's role: climbs the candles with you; perches on the latest candle; plants a flag at each exam candle; slides down a retracement dip when you return for mixed review (spaced repetition made playful, one 800ms beat, then done).  
Vibrant vs professional: the chart-ridge uses brand green, not data green (never reads as a trade); candles-as-platforms keep our hollow/filled language so the world itself reinforces the lesson vocabulary.  
Risk: more custom art and motion work; must stay readable at a glance (zoomed view shows \~8 candles around you; a pinch/mini-map shows the whole journey).

## **What is identical in both**

> * Node states locked/current/done with check \+ label (never colour alone).  
> * "Up next" card, streak and XP chips, module intro state, mastery matrix link.  
> * Bull placement rules (onboarding, empty states, milestones, journey companion) and animation budgets from the motion spec.  
> * WCAG AA, reduced-motion variants, 48px targets.
