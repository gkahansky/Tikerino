# Tikerino Journey Path - Two Concepts for Guy's Pick (19 Sep 2026)

**Ask (Guy, 19/9):** build a spatial map/path; suggest 2 concepts with mockups to decide.

## Concept A - The Trail (proven pattern)
One winding vertical trail per module, scrolling bottom-to-top. Lesson nodes are round stepping stones; the next lesson pulses gently. The bull stands at your current stone and walks ahead on completion. Milestones are landmarks: a gate for mixed review, a summit flag for the exam. Between modules a small bridge with the module's name.
Why: the Duolingo pattern; zero learning curve; nodes map 1:1 to the content model; small Engineering effort.
Risk: reads as a "Duolingo clone" if the art is generic.

## Concept B - The Chart Climb (brand-distinctive)
The journey is itself a rising chart. Each module is one upward leg of a long candlestick chart drawn as a mountain ridge; lessons are the candles you climb. Milestones are marked candles (tall wick for the exam); completed modules form a visible uptrend; a dashed future line shows the path ahead.
Why: unmistakably Tikerino; teaches the product's core metaphor; screenshot-worthy.
Bull: climbs candles, perches on the latest, plants a flag at each exam candle, slides down a retracement dip for mixed review (one 800ms beat).
Risk: more custom art and motion; must stay readable (zoomed view ~8 candles; pinch/mini-map for the whole journey).

## Identical in both
Node states locked/current/done with check + label (never colour alone); "Up next" card, streak and XP chips, module intro state, mastery matrix link; bull placement rules; WCAG AA, reduced motion, 48px targets.
