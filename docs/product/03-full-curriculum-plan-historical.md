# **Tikerino \- Full Curriculum Plan**

*MVP to sellable product. Drafted 2026-09-13 by Instinct. Status: proposal for Guy's review.*

## **1\. Headline numbers**

> * **12 modules, 96 lessons, 96 videos** (one 45-90s narrated animated video per lesson; some up to \~2 min).  
> * **Free tier: modules 1-3 \= 24 lessons/videos**, fully accessible before registration (value-before-registration, per PlanB market research).  
> * **Premium tier: modules 4-12 \= 72 lessons/videos.**  
> * **Total production cost for the full catalog: \$104-328 (midpoint \~\$215) \= ₪315-995 (midpoint \~₪655)** at 1 USD \= 3.03 ILS.  
> * **Amortized average: \~\$1.10-3.40 per video** (₪3.30-10.30). Marginal cost per additional video: \$0.15-0.60.  
> * Comparison point from the market research: incumbents pay \$800-3,000 per lesson of produced educational content; this pipeline lands under \$4 per video amortized at catalog scale.  
> * Sellable product (free tier complete \+ first 3 premium modules) exists at the end of Phase 4: **48 videos, cumulative spend \~\$97-300**.

## **2\. Grounding**

> * Content pack v0.2 (12 Sep 2026): 9 lessons live across 2 topics \- Trading fundamentals (6) and Candlestick reading (3). Architecture doc plans narration for all 16 lessons of these two topics. Guy, 13 Sep: "currently two chapters of 10-20 videos."  
> * PlanB AI market research (12 Sep 2026): 12-module launch curriculum; full access to the first three modules before any paywall or registration; premium \$7/month monthly, \$12-20/month-equivalent annual; launch modules anchored to the 10 highest-search-volume historical market events (2008 crisis, dot-com bubble, COVID crash, GameStop squeeze, etc.).  
> * Video pipeline design doc (13 Sep 2026, Route A Remotion): marginal \$0.15-0.60/video, one-time setup \$50-150, revision reserve \$20-60 per early batch, program total for 16 videos \$75-220, amortized \$5-14/video over 16, falling toward marginal as the catalog grows.  
> * MVP locks that shape the curriculum: English only; WCAG 2.1 AA; freemium with fundamentals free and advanced content paid; synthetic generated charts (no market-data license needed).

## **3\. Curriculum structure: 12 modules x 8 lessons \= 96 videos**

Each lesson is the existing loop (principle card \+ guided example \+ graded exercise) plus one 45-90s narrated animated video rendered by the Remotion pipeline from the lesson JSON. Module exams are exercises, not videos. \[shipped\] \= lesson already live in content pack v0.2; \[16-plan\] \= completes the architecture doc's 16-lesson plan; \[new\] \= new content.

## **4\. Production cost model (full catalog, 96 videos)**

FX: 1 USD \= 3.03 ILS. All numbers from the video pipeline design doc's Route A cost model, scaled from 16 to 96 videos.

| Line | Basis | USD | ILS |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Marginal cost per video | Claude script \+ storyboard (\$0.10-0.30), Nova TTS (\$0.02-0.05), render (\$0-0.30), hosting (\~\$0.01) | \$0.15-0.60 | ₪0.45-1.80 |
| One-time pipeline setup | Remotion project, 5-8 scene templates, bull pose library, storyboard schema, render script; 3-7 focused days; API spend | \$50-150 | ₪150-455 |
| Licenses | Remotion free up to 3-person org; OpenAI and Claude usage-billed | \$0 | ₪0 |
| Revision reserve (whole catalog) | Re-renders while templates settle; front-loaded on the first 16 videos, tapering after | \$40-120 | ₪120-365 |
| Marginal total, 96 videos | 96 x \$0.15-0.60 | \$14-58 | ₪44-175 |
| **Program total** | Setup \+ reserve \+ marginal | **\$104-328 (mid \~\$215)** | **₪315-995 (mid \~₪655)** |
| Amortized per video | Program total / 96 | \$1.10-3.40 | ₪3.30-10.30 |

Guy's review time: 5-10 minutes per video \= 8-16 hours across the catalog, not a dollar cost. Optional add-on, not in baseline: cloned voice via ElevenLabs at \$11/month if Nova ever feels too generic. Later content edits re-render a video for \$0.15-0.60 each.

**Sellable point: end of Phase 4\.** 24 free videos (value-before-registration hook per the research) \+ 24 premium videos behind the \$7-20/month tier \= 48 videos total, cumulative spend \~\$97-300 (₪295-910). Phases 5-6 add depth for retention and annual-plan attachment, not launch readiness.  
Alternative worth considering: the research recommends anchoring launch modules to the 10 most-searched historical market events for SEO and shareability. This plan keeps market history as premium module 10; if organic acquisition matters more than premium depth at launch, pull module 10 forward into Phase 4 and push module 6 (chart patterns) later.
