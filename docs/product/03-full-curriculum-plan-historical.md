# Tikerino - Full Curriculum Plan (historical, 13 Sep 2026)
*MVP to sellable product. Status at the time: proposal for Guy's review.*

## 1. Headline numbers
- 12 modules, 96 lessons, 96 videos (one 45-90s narrated animated video per lesson).
- Free tier: modules 1-3 = 24 lessons; Premium: modules 4-12 = 72 lessons.
- Total production cost for the full catalog: $104-328 (midpoint ~$215). Amortized ~$1.10-3.40 per video; marginal $0.15-0.60.
- Sellable product (free tier + first 3 premium modules) at the end of Phase 4: 48 videos, cumulative spend ~$97-300.

## 2. Grounding (as of 13 Sep)
- Content pack v0.2: 9 lessons live across 2 topics - Trading fundamentals (6) and Candlestick reading (3). Architecture doc planned narration for all 16 lessons of these two topics.
- PlanB market research (12 Sep): 12-module launch curriculum; first three modules before paywall/registration; premium $7/month, $12-20/month-equivalent annual.
- MVP locks: English only; WCAG 2.1 AA; synthetic generated charts.

## 3. Structure: 12 modules x 8 lessons
Each lesson is the existing loop (principle card + guided example + graded exercise) plus one narrated animated video. Module exams are exercises.

Module 1 - Trading Fundamentals: Meet the chart [shipped]; Price moves and percent change [shipped]; Volume [shipped]; Timeframes [shipped]; Bid, ask, and spread [shipped]; Orders, long and short [shipped]; What a stock is [16-plan]; Market cap, sectors, and indices [16-plan].
Module 2 - Candlestick Reading: Anatomy of a candle [shipped]; Bullish and bearish candles [shipped]; Body size, range, and honest limits [shipped]; Wicks and shadows [16-plan]; Doji [16-plan]; Marubozu [16-plan]; Gaps [16-plan]; Reading two and three candles together [16-plan].
Module 3 - Classic Candlestick Patterns (new): Hammer/hanging man; Inverted hammer/shooting star; Engulfing; Harami/inside bars; Morning/evening star; Three white soldiers/three black crows; Tweezer tops/bottoms; When patterns fail.
Modules 4-12 (premium): Trends and Trendlines; Support and Resistance; Chart Patterns; Volume and Indicators; Risk and Money Management; Entries, Exits, and Practice; Market History's Greatest Charts; Investor Psychology; Putting It All Together.

## 4-5. Cost model and phases (summary)
Marginal per video: script + storyboard (Claude) $0.10-0.30, TTS (Nova) $0.02-0.05, render $0-0.30, hosting ~$0.01. One-time Remotion pipeline setup $50-150 (3-7 days). Phases: (1) done - modules 1-2 content partly live; (2) video pipeline + modules 1-2 (16 videos); (3) complete free tier (24); (4) sellable premium launch (48); (5) indicators/risk/entries (72); (6) full catalog (96).

## 6. Open decisions at the time
Module count/depth; where market-history module sits; module 3 lesson sign-off; premium price point.
